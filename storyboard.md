## 3b: app flow

1. Home: farm health score, top suggestion/prediction
2. Map: probes, coloured by status
3. Probe: live readings, battery/device health
4. Live: drop probe in dirty water → reading spikes → score drops → alert
5. Alert: AI diagnosis + suggested action

## 4: value

- Safety (farmers, animals)
- More produce
- Time saved
- Money saved on fines, compliance proof
- Less usage of materials
